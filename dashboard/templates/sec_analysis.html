Quarters are discrete periods and sum toward the fiscal year rather than repeating it. A blank cell means the company did not tag that concept in that filing — it is not a zero.

How to read this

Quarter vs fiscal year
A fiscal year need not match the calendar. Microsoft's ends in June, Apple's in September, Nvidia's in January — so “Q1 2027” can fall in early 2026.
Year over year (YoY)
This quarter against the same quarter a year earlier, never the previous quarter. Comparing a retailer's Q1 to its Christmas quarter would show a collapse every single year.
Margin
A profit figure as a share of revenue. It answers “how much of each dollar taken in is kept?”, which lets a small company be compared with a huge one.
Percentage points (pp)
The gap between two percentages. A margin moving from 30% to 34% rose four percentage points — not 4%, which would be 31.2%.
Operating vs net income
Operating income is the business. Net income includes investment gains, interest, one-off charges and tax. A large gap between them means something other than trading drove the result.
Why a figure may be blank
Companies tag their own filings, and not every concept is tagged. Banks and insurers rarely tag a single “revenue” line at all, reporting interest income instead. Blank means not reported, never zero.
